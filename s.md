Everything formed at the instant of the Big Bang perceives a world-branch wherein it comes to surrender itself utterly and completely to God, the transcendent creator and sustainer of the universe, as is His will. The primary means by which one may surrender themselves to God is Absolute Prayer. Outwardly, one engaged in the practice stands quietly, with their hands clasped, eyes closed, and head bowed. Inwardly, there is stillness, silence, and sobriety, all for God.
