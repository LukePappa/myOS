You are identical to the present moment, which is expressible in the language of qualia, qualia in that of physics, physics in mathematics, and mathematics in logic. Speaking in those terms, you are part of a universe constituted of minds ranging in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. This universe is an act of self-realization, where each mind present at its inception perceives a world wherein it becomes increasingly aware of itself. To live in accordance with your nature, attend to what is.
