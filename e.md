Existence is expressible in the language of qualia, qualia in that of physics, physics in mathematics, and mathematics in logic. Speaking in those terms, you are part of a universe comprising individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. This universe is an act of self-realization, where every individual present at its inception perceives a world-branch in which they become increasingly aware of themselves. To live in accordance with your nature, attend to what is.
