Direct reality can be expressed in the language of qualia; qualia, in that of physics; physics, in that of mathematics; and mathematics, in that of logic. Speaking in those terms, you are part of a universe that comprises individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. This universe is an act of self-realization, where every individual present at its inception perceives a world in which they become increasingly aware of themselves. To live in accordance with your nature, seek what is.
