I am identical to the present moment, which can be expressed in the language of qualia; qualia, in that of physics; physics, in that of mathematics; and mathematics, in that of logic. Speaking in those terms, I inhabit a universe comprising individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. This universe is an act of self-realization, where every individual present at its inception perceives a world in which it becomes increasingly aware of itself. To live in accordance with my nature, I will attend to what is.
