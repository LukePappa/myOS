Existence is undivided, unchanging, and boundless. Its appearance is expressible in the language of qualia, qualia in that of physics, physics in mathematics, and mathematics in logic. Speaking in those terms, you are part of a universe comprising individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. This universe is an act of self-realization, where every individual present at its inception perceives a world-branch in which it becomes increasingly aware of itself. To live in accordance with your nature, attend to what is.
