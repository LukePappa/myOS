Existence is eternal, indivisible, unchanging, boundless, and complete. Its appearance is expressible in the language of qualia; qualia, in that of physics; physics, in that of mathematics; and mathematics, in that of logic. Speaking in those terms, you inhabit a universe that comprises individuals who range in complexity from singular particles to entire nervous systems. This universe is an act of self-realization, where every individual formed at the instant of the Big Bang perceives a world-branch in which it becomes increasingly aware of itself. To live in accordance with your purpose, be present.
